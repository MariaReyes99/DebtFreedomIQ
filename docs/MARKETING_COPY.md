## Positioning
**DebtFreedomIQ is your financial triage and decision-support platform.**
Not a budgeting app. People in hardship don't primarily need budgeting; they need clarity, options and a pathway to help.

- **Clarity:** a free 10-minute check shows where you stand and how long your savings last.
- **Options:** a dated plan, and side-by-side comparisons before big decisions (sell, refinance, rent out, downsize, restructure).
- **A pathway to help:** the free services near you, sorted by urgency, and a referral package so the first appointment counts.

## Launch plan (first 90 days)
1. **Weeks 1–2: soft launch.** Friends, family and your course network do the check and give feedback through Contact support. Fix what's confusing.
2. **Weeks 2–6: partners.** Contact 10 financial-mentoring services, community law centres, Citizens Advice Bureaux and student support teams. Offer free access and ask for feedback, not money.
3. **Weeks 4–8: content.** Post the three designs weekly; write short guides ("What to do when you get a disconnection notice") that link to the free check.
4. **Weeks 6–12: proof.** Ask users who made progress to share a story; publish approved ones on Pricing. Track: checks completed, people reaching free help, gaps closed.
5. **After 3 months:** decide on Premium Plus pricing (consider NZ$79–99/year once there is evidence and testimonials), and approach one employer or bank hardship team with results.
